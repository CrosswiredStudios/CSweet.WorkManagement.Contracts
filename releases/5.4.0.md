# 5.4.0

Adds per-ticket verification requirements, declared once by planning.

- `WorkItemPlanningSpecification.Verifications` (`IReadOnlyList<WorkItemVerificationRequirement>?`). `null` means the ticket was planned before 5.4.0, so consumers may keep their legacy rules. An empty list means planning declared that no special verification is required. Not written to JSON when `null`.
- `WorkItemVerificationRequirement(Kind)` with `RequireCanvas`, `RequireWebGl` and `ViewportWidths`.
- `WorkItemVerificationKinds.BrowserSmoke` (`browser-smoke`): launch the built web output in real Chromium and record page errors, failed assets, the visible surface and a screenshot.
- `WorkItemVerificationKinds.BrowserSmokeReceiptCommand` (`node .csweet/browser-smoke.cjs`): the standard receipt an author binds to its validations, followed by the build directory and `--canvas` when required.
- `WorkItemVerifications`: `IsDeclared`, `BrowserSmoke`, `IsBrowserSmokeReceipt` and `Validate` (known kinds, each kind once, at most four requirements, at most four distinct viewport widths of 320–3840 px).

Why: on 2026-11-10 the Engineer, the Technical Director's reviewer and QA each decided from different text whether a ticket needed browser evidence. A ticket went back to engineering although its code was approved.

Additive wire contract release; older consumers ignore the field. Publish before the platform, Technical Director, Producer, Engineer and QA releases that use it.

Validation: not yet run. New tests: `WorkItemVerificationTests`, `BrowserSmokeReceiptTests`.
